# 🎸 ProMapper Manifesto & Vibe

> "Software with a soul that does ONE thing: turns talk into shape."

ProMapper is not a productivity tool. It is a **workshop table**. Most apps force you to adapt to their structure—create the folder, name the document, format the headings. ProMapper takes the absolute messiest form of human input (a scattered rant, a 4-person argument, an ongoing jam session) and automatically gives it a spine.

It cures the headache of "Wait, what did we actually agree on?" without requiring anyone to be the designated note-taker.

## The Pitch (Three Flavors)

### The Anti-Corporate One-Liner
> *"Turn messy conversations into a living project map that actually keeps up with you."*

### The 15-Second Elevator Pitch
"Most note-taking apps are just digital filing cabinets, but creative projects don't happen in a straight line. **ProMapper is a workshop table for your messy thoughts.** 
You just drop in a voice note, a rant, or a meeting recording, and it instantly shapes the chaos into a living map—extracting action items, summarizing the big picture, and building a visual web of topics you can push around. 
But the real magic is that it has a memory. Add another conversation next week, and it folds the new context right into the same board, quietly checking off the tasks you said you finished. It’s an intelligent, self-organizing memory for whatever you're circling."

### The "Why It Wins" Pitch
"It’s a local-first, collaborative canvas that does one thing perfectly: it turns unstructured audio and text into structured momentum. You don't have to format anything. You don't have to start a new document for a new meeting. You just append your latest thoughts, and the AI reconciles it against the existing map—updating your action items and evolving the topic graph in real-time. It runs entirely on your own API keys, zero subscriptions, and wrapped in a pastel aesthetic that actually feels fun to use. It’s weaponized simplicity."

## 🧬 Core Tenets

1. **The Append-Reconcile Loop is the Moat.**
   Linear transcripts are obsolete for ongoing projects. Saying *"I finished writing that report"* should quietly tick the "Write report" item ✓ on the board. The map keeps up with you.
   
2. **Local-First, Anti-Scale.**
   No SaaS dashboards, no enterprise RBAC, no data harvesting. "Can't scale IS the feature." It respects privacy by design. A tool that runs locally, on your own keys, with zero friction.

3. **Soft Neo-Toybrut Aesthetic.**
   Pastel mesh gradients, chunky UI elements, and tactile Web Audio sound FX (chimes, ticks, portal whooshes). B2B software looks like an airport. ProMapper looks and feels like an Amiga demoscene had a baby with a pastel candy shop. It doesn't take itself too seriously.

4. **Zero Friction Onboarding.**
   No signup wall, no tutorial modals, no "What is your role?" survey. Just drop a voice note and let the magic happen.

5. **Talk Without Ceremony.**
   Words stay out of the way, color carries identity, the dice makes the vibe yours. 

*"The best code feels like magic but works like clockwork"*
